# Ad Creative Brief

## Start from what already works

The best first ads are usually the owner's top organic posts (from `learnings.md` and reports), lightly adapted. Then add new angles from `susan-competitor-ads` gaps.

## The test matrix

Plan 3 to 5 angles × 1 or 2 formats. One variable changes at a time so results are readable.

| Angle | What it says | Example opening |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Names the pain in the client's words | "Still answering every enquiry yourself at 10pm?" |
| Desire | Paints the outcome | |
| Proof | A real result from `proof.md` | |
| Objection | Answers the biggest reason they hesitate | |
| Offer | The clear, simple invitation | |

Formats: talking-head video, screen-share demo, carousel, single image, testimonial clip.

## Copy for each ad

- **Opening line:** stops the right person, repels the wrong one.
- **Primary text:** problem → why the usual fix fails → what's different here → proof → one clear next step. Short version and long version.
- **Headline** (under about 40 characters) and **description**.
- **CTA** matching the landing page's action exactly.
- **Message match:** the ad's promise, words and visual should be the first thing the landing page shows.

## Visual brief for each ad

Format and size, what's on screen, the text on the image (short), which real photo or footage, and what the first second shows.

## Refresh signals

Rising cost per result with steady targeting, falling click-through rate, or the same people seeing it many times: replace the creative, keep the winning angle.

## Rules

- Voice per `voice.md`. No claims beyond `proof.md`. No fake urgency or scarcity.
- Follow platform ad policies (no personal-attribute call-outs like "Are you struggling with debt?", no before/after health claims).
- Never AI images of real people.
