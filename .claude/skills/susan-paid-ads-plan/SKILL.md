# Paid Ads Plan

Ads amplify what already works. The best plan starts from the business's real numbers and its best organic posts, not from a budget figure.

## 1. Foundations (ask before planning)

- **Goal:** one primary result (sales, booked calls, applications, sign-ups, event tickets).
- **The numbers:** price, profit per sale, current conversion rate of the landing page, how many leads become customers. Use `susan-ad-math` to set a target cost per result.
- **Tracking:** is the platform's pixel or conversion tracking installed and firing on the result page? No tracking, no ads: fix this first.
- **Landing page:** does it match the ad's promise? Run `susan-landing-page-review` if unsure.
- **Proof and creative:** which organic posts performed best? Those are the first ad candidates.

## 2. Platform choice

- **Meta (Facebook, Instagram):** broad consumer and small-business audiences, strong for lead forms and low-ticket offers.
- **TikTok:** reach and younger audiences; needs native-feeling video.
- **LinkedIn:** B2B and professionals; expensive per click, strong for high-value offers.
- **Google Search:** captures people already searching; strong when demand exists.
Start with one platform. Add a second only when the first is profitable.

## 3. Structure (keep it simple)

- **Test phase:** one campaign, one or two audiences (broad plus one interest or lookalike), 3 to 5 creatives, each with a different angle (problem, proof, objection, offer).
- **Retargeting:** people who visited, watched or engaged, shown proof and objection-handling creative.
- **Scale phase:** move budget to winning creatives and audiences; add new angles to replace tired ones.

## 4. Budget and timeline

- Minimum test budget: enough for each ad set to reach about 50 results, or at least several times the target cost per result, over 7 to 14 days.
- Don't judge an ad in its first 2 to 3 days unless it's clearly failing.
- Scale winners gradually (about 20% budget increases every few days) so delivery stays stable.

## 5. Output

A one-page plan: goal and target cost per result, tracking status, platform, campaign structure, audiences, creative list (angle, format, source post), budget and dates, what counts as a win, and the next decision date.

## Rules

- No income or result claims in ads that `proof.md` can't back. Follow each platform's ad policies (especially health, finance and personal attributes).
- Launch everything paused for the owner to review. Never spend money without their explicit approval.
