# A/B Test

## Designing a test

- **Hypothesis:** "Changing X to Y will increase Z because of reason." One sentence.
- **One variable:** opening line, image, offer framing, CTA, headline or form length. Never several at once.
- **One success metric,** decided before the test starts (saves, click-through rate, cost per lead, sign-up rate).
- **Equal conditions:** same audience, budget, time period and placement.
- **Stopping rule:** decide in advance how much data or time is needed (for ads, often at least 7 days and a meaningful number of results per version).

## Judging results

- **Small numbers mislead.** 3 sign-ups vs 5 is not a winner. If each version has only a handful of results, the honest verdict is "not enough data yet".
- **Rough check:** for conversion rates, a difference is more believable when each side has at least about 100 conversions, or when the gap is large and stays steady for a full week. For a proper answer, calculate statistical significance (aim for 95% confidence) and show the inputs.
- **Watch the whole chain:** a version with more clicks but worse buyers isn't the winner.
- **Organic posts** can't be split-tested cleanly (different days, different audiences). Treat organic comparisons as signals and confirm patterns over several weeks.

## Output

The hypothesis, the setup, a results table, the verdict (winner / no clear winner / not enough data), confidence in plain words, and what to test next. Add proven wins to `learnings.md` through the analyst.
