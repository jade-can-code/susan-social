# Canva Production

## Why templates beat generation

Generated designs drift: fonts, spacing and layouts change every time, and the feed starts to look like a stock catalogue. A small set of the owner's own templates, filled with their own photos, is what makes a feed recognizable and human.

## One-time setup (ask the owner to do this in Canva)

1. **Brand Kit:** colors (hex), fonts, logo versions (light and dark).
2. **A templates folder** (for example "Susan Templates") with one design per repeat format, and 2 or 3 layout variations of each:
   - single graphic or tip card (1080×1350)
   - carousel: cover, 3 inside slides, closing CTA slide (1080×1350)
   - quote or mock social post card (1080×1350)
   - video cover (1080×1920)
   - story (1080×1920)
   Name text boxes by role (HEADLINE, HIGHLIGHT, BODY, CTA) so filling them is unambiguous.
3. **A photos folder** (for example "Susan Photos") with 30 to 50 real photos: the owner at work, workspace, process, products, everyday life, plus black-and-white versions if the brand uses them.
4. If the Canva plan supports Brand Templates with data fields, publish the templates that way; autofill is then the cleanest path.

## Producing a design

1. Search the templates folder for the format. Copy the template.
2. Replace text with the exact approved words. Keep one highlighted word per slide in the brand accent color.
3. Swap in a real photo from the photos folder that matches the brief.
4. No template for the format? Generate with the Brand Kit, choose the strongest candidate, and tell the owner to save it as a new template.
5. Export PNG (one per slide). Check the export: spelling exact, fonts and colors correct, nothing crowding the logo, text inside safe margins (about 8% from each edge).

## Making it look human

- Real photos over stock; stock over AI. Never AI images of real people.
- Rotate layout variations; vary crops and photo choices week to week.
- Use real screenshots of the owner's actual work (notes, dashboards, process) where relevant.
- Keep text short: one idea per slide, large type, high contrast.
- Leave breathing room. Clutter reads as template.

## Limits

- Text always set in Canva, never baked into an AI image.
- Never share, publish or schedule from Canva on the owner's behalf.
- Some features depend on the Canva plan. If a tool fails 3 times, stop and report it.
