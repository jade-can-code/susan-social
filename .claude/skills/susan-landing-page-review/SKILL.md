# Landing Page Review

Read the page (fetch the URL) on mobile width first; most social traffic is mobile.

## The checks

1. **Message match:** does the headline repeat the promise of the ad or post that sent people here? Same words, same offer, same look. Mismatch is the most common leak.
2. **5-second test:** can a stranger tell what it is, who it's for and what to do next within 5 seconds?
3. **One goal:** one primary action. Remove competing links and buttons.
4. **The ideal client's words:** does the copy name their problem the way they'd say it (`audience.md`)?
5. **Proof near the ask:** real testimonials, results, faces and names (with permission), placed beside the buttons.
6. **Objections answered:** price, time, "will it work for me", "what if I fail". An FAQ helps.
7. **Form friction:** only the fields truly needed. Every extra field costs sign-ups. Explain what happens after submitting.
8. **Mobile:** readable text, tappable buttons, the button visible without much scrolling, nothing broken.
9. **Speed:** slow pages lose visitors. Note very large images or videos.
10. **Trust:** contact details, privacy note, secure checkout, real person behind it.
11. **The thank-you page:** confirms what happens next and gives one next step (book, watch, join, share).
12. **Tracking:** the conversion event fires on the thank-you or confirmation page.
13. **Lead quality:** does the page qualify people (who it's for and not for), or invite anyone?

## Output

- A score per check (good / needs work / broken) with one line each.
- **Top 5 fixes** ranked by impact ÷ effort, each with the exact suggested change (new headline, cut field, moved testimonial).
- **2 or 3 test ideas** for `susan-ab-test`, each with a hypothesis.

## Rules

- Suggest copy in the brand's voice. No claims beyond `proof.md`.
- Don't edit the live page; hand the owner the changes.
