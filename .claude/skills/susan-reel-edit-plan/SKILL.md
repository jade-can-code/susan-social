# Reel Edit Plan

## The edit blueprint

Build a table, one row per beat (roughly one spoken line):

`Time | Spoken line | Beat role | On screen | Visual | Caption emphasis | Cut / move`

**Beat roles:** opening (stop the scroll), setup (name the problem), turn (the insight), proof (show it), step (what to do), CTA.

### Rules for each beat
- **Opening (0 to 2 s):** the most surprising or specific line first. If the raw take starts slowly, cut straight to the strongest line (cold open).
- **Change something every 2 to 4 seconds:** a cut, a punch-in zoom, a cutaway, or new on-screen text. Movement keeps attention; chaos loses it.
- **Show, don't just say:** wherever the script names a thing, plan footage of that thing.
- **Captions on, always:** most people watch muted. Word-by-word or short phrases, one key word highlighted in a brand color, inside the platform's safe zone (keep clear of the bottom 20% and right edge).
- **End on the step or CTA,** not on "thanks for watching".

## Cutaway footage library

Keep a simple folder of reusable clips the owner films once and reuses:
- workspace and hands at work, screen recordings of their real process, products, before/after, everyday life, location shots.
Tag each clip in a `footage-index.md` (file, what it shows, length, mood, topics). Match clips to beats by topic; list any beat with no matching clip as a "footage gap" for the next filming session.

## Audio

- Never change the meaning of what was said. Basic clean-up (noise, levels) only with the owner's OK.
- Voice-only works for teaching. Music sits well under the voice.
- Trending sounds must be added inside the platform app; mark those videos "post natively".
- Never clone or generate the owner's voice.

## Cover and versions

- Cover: a clear frame plus a short headline from the brand's cover template.
- Note per-platform trims (length limits, caption placement, on-screen text near platform buttons).

## Checklist before handing off

- [ ] Opening lands in 2 seconds
- [ ] Something changes every 2 to 4 seconds
- [ ] Captions planned, safe zone respected
- [ ] Every named thing has a visual or a footage gap listed
- [ ] Cover and audio specified
- [ ] 45 seconds target, 60 max (unless long-form is the goal)
