# Performance Report

## Principles

- **Their goals, not generic ones.** Report against the KPIs and targets in `goals-and-success.md`. Numbers they said don't matter go at the bottom or nowhere.
- **Real numbers only.** Missing data is "no data", never an estimate.
- **Trends beat snapshots.** Compare with last period and the 4-week average.
- **Explain, then recommend.** Every chart or table earns one sentence of meaning.

## Structure

1. **Headline (3 lines):** the result that matters most, the biggest win, the biggest problem.
2. **KPI table:** KPI · this period · last period · target · on track?
3. **Content:** top 3 and bottom 3 posts with the pattern behind each (opening, format, pillar, platform, time).
4. **Funnel:** reach → engagement → profile visits / clicks → leads → sales, where data exists. Where does it leak?
5. **Ads (if running):** spend, cost per result vs target, best and worst creative. Use `susan-ad-math` for the calculations.
6. **Next actions:** 3 at most, each specific, owned and dated.
7. **Questions for the owner:** anything needed, scheduled for their next work day.

## Useful signals

- Saves and shares show intent to use or pass on; comments show conversation; profile visits and link clicks show interest in the business.
- Reach without saves usually means the opening worked but the content didn't deliver.
- High saves with few clicks means valuable content with a weak or missing next step.

## Tone

Plain, calm and honest. Celebrate real wins; name problems without blame. No jargon without a short explanation.
