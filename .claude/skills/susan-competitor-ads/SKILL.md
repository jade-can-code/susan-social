# Competitor Ads

## Where to look (all public, no login needed)

- **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library): active Facebook and Instagram ads by advertiser or keyword, with start dates.
- **TikTok Creative Center / Top Ads:** high-performing TikTok ads by industry and region.
- **Google Ads Transparency Center:** search and display ads by advertiser.
- **LinkedIn Ad Library:** ads by company.

## Method

1. **List 5 to 10 competitors and adjacent brands** the ideal client also sees (from `audience.md` and the owner's input).
2. **Collect their active ads.** For each: the opening line, the angle, the offer, the format, the call to action, the landing page, and how long it's been running.
3. **Longevity is the signal.** An ad running for weeks or months is very likely profitable; a burst of new variations of one idea means they're testing it.
4. **Group by angle:** pain, desire, proof, objection, urgency, identity, education. Count which angles dominate.
5. **Find the gaps:** objections nobody answers, audiences nobody speaks to, formats nobody uses, proof nobody shows. Gaps are where this business can stand out.

## Output

- A table of the ads reviewed (link, advertiser, angle, offer, format, running since).
- The 3 dominant angles in the market, with examples.
- 3 to 5 gaps, each turned into an angle this business could own, in its own voice.
- Notes on landing pages that look strong.

## Rules

- Learn from the angle and structure; never copy wording, images or offers.
- Report only what you can see. Ad libraries don't show spend or results for most ads, so never state them as fact.
- Never use competitor names or trademarks in this business's ads.
